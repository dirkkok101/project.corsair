# Trading, ship battles, ocean navigation — Black Flag, Sea of Thieves, Sid Meier's Pirates!

Collected 2026-10-09 for Corsair. Designer interviews and deep dives only; no invented quotes.

## Trading

### Black Flag
- Almost no buy-low / sell-high market. Cargo comes from boarding, sinking, diving, harpooning and forts. It pays for Jackdaw upgrades.
- Spyglass shows a ship's hold before you attack. Boarding pays about double sinking.
- Kenway's Fleet: send captured ships on off-screen trade runs. Original needed online and real time (missions could take 24+ hours).
- Remake (Resynced): fleet built into the game; buy/sell at harbourmaster "so you don't enter a fight unprepared"; every prize forces a choice (repair, lower wanted level, add to fleet, captain's lockbox).
- Ismail: pirates board ships and plunder cargo to upgrade the ship ([Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2013/11/ashraf-ismail-of-ubisoft-speaks-on-assassins-creed-iv-black-flag)).
- Steal: show cargo before attacking; "what do I do with this prize?" choice.
- Avoid: economy locked in a separate menu or app cut off from sailing.

### Sea of Thieves
- Cargo is physical on deck. Merchant Alliance runs have deadlines and condition rules.
- Trade Routes (Season Two): Emissaries buy commodities at one outpost and sell where sought after; supply/demand shifts weekly ([release notes 2.1.0](https://www.seaofthieves.com/release-notes/2.1.0)).
- Chapman: while the chest is on the ship "it's not yours… When you cash them in at the outpost that's when… it's safe. There is an in-between, and during that you're meant to feel the paranoia" ([Metro](https://metro.co.uk/2018/02/13/sea-of-thieves-end-game-revealed-rare-interview-with-lead-designer-mike-chapman-7308093/)).
- Rare cut personal-gold loss on sink ("too profound") and crew-on-crew theft (amazing once, then broken).
- Steal: unbanked loot until port; seasonal demand shifts shown in a port ledger.
- Avoid: wiping long-term progress when you lose a ship.

## Ship-to-ship battles

### Black Flag
- Three sail speeds; weapons: side cannons, chain shot, mortar, swivel, fire barrels, ram, brace. Waves as cover.
- Disable then sink (fast) or board (more loot). Wanted level brings hunters. Level-gated enemies; legendary ships as bosses.
- Ismail: realistic sail-and-shoot prototypes were impossible; ship should feel heavy but "not twitchy" ([Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/2013/9/30/4778756/assassins-creed-4-black-flag-and-the-terrible-beauty-of-naval-warfare/)).
- Steal: short readable fights; sink-or-board; visible danger tiers; weather as cover.
- Avoid: progress that is mostly bigger upgrade numbers.

### Sea of Thieves
- No health bar. Holes flood; water sinks the ship. Patch and bail. Later: mast, capstan, wheel damage; repeat hits enlarge holes.
- Boarding is a player action, not a cutscene.
- Chapman: months on everything around cannons before cannons; "they're pirates, they're not marines… Our game isn't about combat dominance."
- Skeleton ships added so fights aren't only PvP ([Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/the-extremely-incomplete-history-of-sea-of-thieves-as-told-by-rare)).
- Steal: flooding as health; damage to specific parts; readable damage.
- Avoid: fights that only work with four coordinated humans. Corsair is single-player: abstract crew into orders.

## Ocean navigation

### Black Flag
- Ocean is the hub; islands are points of interest. Dynamic weather. Remake: Pathfinder + Follow Sea. Wind is a small speed bonus only.
- Ismail: leaving an island is "like you hit a reset button mentally." Tacking cut because it fought with shooting.
- Credible, not realistic ([Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/features/id/17577/article/interview-with-assassin-s-creed-iv-black-flag-s-director-ashraf-ismail/)).
- Steal: dense varied islands; storms as hazard; optional autopilot for long trips.
- Avoid: map clutter / icon checklist sailing.

### Sea of Thieves
- No minimap or waypoints. Chart below decks; compass, landmarks, spyglass. Sail trim vs wind. Crew roles (wheel, map, crow's nest).
- Preston: helmsman shouldn't sail and see the map at once; rocks and nuances must be seen.
- Chapman: "Make it believable, not realistic." Solo sloop added because solo was too hard.
- Steal: read the world (coast, landmarks, chart); wind understandable at a glance.
- Avoid: slow/confusing solo navigation.

## vs Sid Meier's Pirates!
- Sid: career + world map are the game; sailing, towns, duels, battles are short scenes. Mini-games simple enough not to lose the objective ([Rouse](https://flylib.com/books/en/4.479.1.17/1/)).
- Black Flag: weight on boat and open sea; Ismail named Pirates! as the last really great pirate game.
- Sea of Thieves: weight on crew voyage and player-made stories; no career spine.
- All three: heavy realism fights fun. Sid kept wind as battle spice; Black Flag mostly cut it; Sea of Thieves kept sail trim.
- Firaxis: barnacle scraping isn't fun; jail not death so players attempt heroic actions ([Designer Diary #2](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/sid-meiers-pirates-designer-diary-2/1100-6102516/)).

## Corsair recommendations
1. Show a ship's hold before battle. After a win, one screen: cargo, ship, repair, or lower notoriety.
2. Plunder isn't yours until banked in port. Full hold slows you and draws hunters; a sink costs that trip's haul, not the career.
3. Ports have a changing demand table (one surplus, one sought-after), in a ledger and on the map.
4. Fleet missions stay on the main map as moving markers that can be intercepted. Don't hide them in a menu.
5. Battles last 2–4 minutes. Flooding is health; holes, water level, one or two damaged parts. Orders: pumps, patch, guns.
6. Wind matters on the map for route time. In battle, only a weather-gage bonus. No tacking.
7. Navigate by chart, landmarks and weather; optional autopilot for long trips. Don't fill the map with icons.
8. Visible danger tiers. Win upward through ship choice and skill, not only upgrade numbers.
