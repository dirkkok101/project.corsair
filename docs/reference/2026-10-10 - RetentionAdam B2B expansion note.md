# RetentionAdam: MoltSets launch (B2B contact data API)

- **URL:** https://x.com/RetentionAdam/status/2108587290528469468
- **Author:** Adam Robinson (@RetentionAdam), CEO of MoltSets, RB2B and Retention.com
- **Posted:** 9 Oct 2026, 17:56 SAST
- **Links:** https://moltsets.com (card: "B2B Search & Enrichment API for AI Workflows | MoltSets")
- **Engagement at capture:** ~7.9k views, 87 likes, 156 bookmarks, 17 replies

## What it is
Adam Robinson announces MoltSets, an API-only B2B contact database (verified emails, mobiles, LinkedIn data) with flat plans from $27/mo and no UI, built for reps and agentic GTM stacks working out of Claude or ChatGPT. He positions it against ZoomInfo and Apollo, arguing API-only, rate-limited flat pricing will undercut seat-based contact data pricing.

## Key claims from the post
- Five pillars: unlimited APIs, high quality, high coverage, high API rate limits, very low price.
- Plans by rate limit: $27 (individual rep), $97 (early-stage SaaS marketer), $397 (lead-gen agency), $997 (heavy users).
- Plans to re-scrape 47m international LinkedIn profiles and re-validate emails every 3 days, stated as live Nov 1.
- Carrier-verified mobile numbers at 15c each via bolt-on plans from $27/mo.
- No vendor waterfalls; does not store customer data after it is pulled.
