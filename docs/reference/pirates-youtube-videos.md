# Sid Meier's Pirates! — YouTube reference videos

Collected 2026-10-08 for sea battles, world-map sailing and UI reference. Titles and channels verified via YouTube oEmbed and video pages. Times marked (approx.) were read from thumbnail previews and may be off by 5–10 s; other times come from uploader chapters or descriptions. None were watched in full.

**Start here:** the Wii video (UI section) covers battles, boarding, sailing and town screens in 10 minutes, no commentary.

## Sea battles

- [Ship of the Line vs. Ship of the Line #1 (Swashbuckler) HD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Maj8TjrkVd4) — Thrym865, 2004 remake, 3:35. Even big-ship fight, no commentary. Description lists upgrades (chain shot for sails, grape shot for crew, copper plating for turning) and a table of ship guns, crew, turning and speed.
- [Royal Sloop vs. Ship of the Line Speed Penalty (Swashbuckler)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqKZ0tvsQB0) — Thrym865, 2004 remake, 2:45. Crew and rigging damage; enemy loses all crew yet escapes. Description explains when AI captains surrender (no crew, no cannons, no rigging, sometimes heavy hull damage).
- [Royal Sloop vs. Baron Raymondo's War Galleon (Swashbuckler) HD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DOj2JGM7mwA) — Thrym865, 2004 remake, 2:34. Grape shot on crew, then boarding and sword duel: the cannon-to-boarding transition.
- [Live the Life (2004) PC Longplay, No Commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYTgWv-pyjg) — Retro Gameplay Channel, 59:31. Battles at 8:20 (Barque), 13:47 (Sloop of War vs Jack Rackham), 51:23 (Henry Morgan's Large Frigate). Also town menu ~4:49, governor ~5:00–5:40, ship/captain status ~6:40–7:20 (approx.).

## Sailing on the world map

- [Sid Meier's Pirates! (2004) | Swashbuckling sailing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2oZ9XZ_Wzfc) — DarthZombie, 2004 PC, 14:26. Review; "Sailing and naval combat" chapter at 4:41. Notes wind nearly always blows east to west, making eastward travel a struggle.
- [SID MEIER'S PIRATES! Gameplay (No Commentary)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E5DDN6BpwFw) — Shiver Me Pixels!, PC remake, 58:05. Sailing with map HUD ~6:40–7:20, parchment world map ~10:00–10:40 (approx.).
- [Sid Meier's Pirates! longplay, MicroProse 1987, PC/DOS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yRXH47iofNs) — Classic Game Sessions, 3:12:08. Original top-down sailing throughout; opening shows setup and text-menu town screens.
